COMPETITION RULES

Quick reference for the facilitator.

  1. Each team has a maximum of three attempts.
  2. Leaving the track incurs a three-second penalty.
  3. Manual repositioning incurs a three-second penalty.
  4. A declared restart consumes one attempt without adding a time penalty.
  5. Only one operator may control the robot during a run.
  6. The fastest valid adjusted completion time wins.
  7. Tied completion times require a tiebreaker.
